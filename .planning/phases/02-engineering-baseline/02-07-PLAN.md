<objective>

The `/analyze` limit protects the scarce GNews quota (about 100 requests per day on the free plan).
Output: limiter and `LimitedRouter`, `/v1` mount, 429 handler and OpenAPI declaration, proxy-trust switch, settings and environment names, two test modules.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| client -> /analyze and /v1 | Anyone can send requests; /analyze spends scarce upstream quota |
| reverse proxy -> app | Forwarded-for headers are attacker-controlled unless they come from a trusted proxy |
| future routes -> limiter | A route added later could silently be unlimited |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-19 | Denial of service | /analyze quota exhaustion | high | mitigate | Per-IP limit 10 per minute (env-configurable, validated at startup), 429 problem+json with Retry-After, evaluated before the cache and before any upstream call (D-10) |
| T-02-20 | Elevation of privilege | silent fail-open limits on included routers (slowapi issue 281) | high | mitigate | Decorators only (no middleware, no default_limits); LimitedRouter applies the limit to every /v1 route; a probe-route test proves the 429; a handler without `request` fails at registration (D-23) |
| T-02-21 | Spoofing | forged X-Forwarded-For bypassing the limit | high | mitigate | Proxy headers are ignored unless TRUST_PROXY_HEADERS=true and then only from FORWARDED_ALLOW_IPS peers; tests cover flag off, flag on trusted peer, flag on untrusted peer (D-10) |
