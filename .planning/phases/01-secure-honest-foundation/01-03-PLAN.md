<objective>
Define the response contract (envelope, results, provenance meta, problem details) and produce the one place demo data may come from: a committed, deterministic, clearly synthetic fixture generated from the unmodified simulator.

Defining the models here (interface first) lets plan 01-05 wire the route without inventing types, and generating the fixture now means Phase 2 can delete the simulator without changing demo behaviour. This plan runs after 01-02 (Task 1's not-ignored check needs 01-02's `.gitignore`, and every task runs 01-02's credential backstop before its commit) and in parallel with 01-04 in wave 3 (no shared files).
Output: extended `models.py`, `scripts/generate_demo_fixture.py`, `fixtures/demo_analyze.json`, two test modules.
</objective>
